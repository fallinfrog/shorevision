    About this data

    Lakes and shorelines: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Coverage and names vary; if a lake is missing it may not be mapped yet.

    Weather: US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). Pressure trend comes from the nearest reporting station.

    Water temperature: estimated from air temperature and season, not measured. Adjust it if you know better.

    Depth: not available for most lakes, so spots are ranked from shoreline shape, not a depth map. This is an advisor, not a fish finder.

    Safety: check local fishing rules, regulations and land access. Stay off the water in storms.
